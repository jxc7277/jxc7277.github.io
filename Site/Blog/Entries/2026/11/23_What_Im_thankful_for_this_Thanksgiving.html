My Blog

 
 
 
 
 

I am going to be on a break during this Thanksgiving break as I spend time with my family. Before I leave, I want to type up a little blog post on what I’m thankful for and get it posted before I log off my computer. To start this off, I want to say that I am very thankful for all of my fellow blog readers and I hope that you have a wonderful thanksgiving with your friends and your family. I cannot wait to return after this break and prepare for the other holidays soon but I want to take this break so that I am not consumed by just being on the internet during this time.


The main thing that I am grateful for is my friends and the memories I’ve shared with them. A good memory I can think of is over the past summer when I had my friends Felix and Charlie show up to my house for my birthday party. I’ve also had good memories talking to other friends like Tupac and George on the phone through texting and calling. All my friends are very supportive of me and I’ve always felt like I could rely on them for anything. I also feel like I could lean on them during times of hardship just like they’ve leaned on me during their hardships.


I feel like this year I also have to be thankful for my family. I love them a lot and I appreciate their company. My biggest joy is getting to be around my parents every day, visiting my grandma, and visiting my brother. This is one my longer entries but I feel like that’s mainly because I’m going to be gone so I don’t just want to leave a shorter entry here. Spend as much time as you can with your friends and family this upcoming day and I will see you all when I get back from my break. Stay safe everybody. Bye!


Sincerely,

Jada Castaneda
